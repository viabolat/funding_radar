# Funding Radar — Pricing Strategy & Monetization Model

## Executive Summary

**Funding Radar** is an AI-powered funding intelligence and eligibility platform designed for non-governmental organizations (NGOs), small and medium enterprises (SMEs), grant writing consultancies, and research institutions in Romania and the European Union.

The platform's monetization model is built on **ROI-Based Value Pricing**. A single successful grant application yields between **€50,000 and €2,000,000+** in funding. In this context, an annual investment of **€2,388 to €9,588** for real-time intelligence, automated applicant guide analysis (*Ghidul Solicitantului*), and instant corrigenda tracking provides an extraordinary **50x to 500x ROI** for active grant seekers and consulting agencies.

## 2. Detailed Tier Breakdown

### Tier 1: Community / Grassroots
- **Target Audience**: Early-stage NGOs, local volunteer associations, small cultural/community groups.
- **Price**: **€29 / month** (or **€290 / year**) — *Optional Free Community Plan available for verified micro-NGOs*.
- **Target Customer Persona**: Volunteer directors, local community organizers who search for funding ad-hoc.

#### Feature Set
- **Weekly Digest**: Summarized email digest of newly published grants delivered every Monday.
- **Web Dashboard Access**: Standard directory search with manual keyword and domain filtering.
- **Single User Seat**: 1 user login per organization profile.
- **Source Coverage**: MIPE public feeds, basic EU SEDIA calls, adieuronest.ro CSV feed.
- **Basic Keyword Matching**: Flat category matching without advanced AI eligibility scoring.

---

### Tier 2: Professional NGO & SME
- **Target Audience**: Established NGOs (health, social care, environment, education) and SMEs seeking digital transformation, R&D, green energy, or regional operational grants (ADR Nord-Vest, PNRR, PEO, POS, AFM).
- **Price**: **€199 / month** (or **€1,990 / year** — save ~17%).
- **Target Customer Persona**: Dedicated Grant Managers, Executive Directors, Innovation Officers.

#### Feature Set
- **Daily & Instant Alerts**: Real-time notifications for newly published calls and immediate corrigenda/addenda alerts.
- **AI Applicant Guide Breakdown (*Ghidul Solicitantului*)**: Automated AI extraction of eligible applicant types, CAEN codes, co-financing percentages, and non-reimbursable caps from 80+ page guideline PDFs.
- **Automated Eligibility Match**: Personalized match scoring based on organization profile (`profile.eligible_as`, mission statement, legal entity status).
- **Team Triage Workspace**: Multi-user collaboration with status workflow (`new`, `relevant`, `applied`, `not_relevant`, `review`) and user assignment.
- **Deadline Reminders & Export**: Configurable alert sequences (e.g., 30, 14, and 3 days before cutoff) with iCal/Google Calendar export.
- **Seats**: Up to 5 team member seats per organization.

---

### Tier 3: Consultancy & Agency
- **Target Audience**: Professional grant writing consultancies, regional business development agencies, and independent fundraising advisors managing portfolios of client NGOs and SMEs.
- **Price**: **€499 / month** (or **€4,990 / year** — save ~17%).
- **Target Customer Persona**: Managing Directors of Grant Consultancies, Senior Fundraisers.

#### Feature Set
- **Multi-Tenant Client Portfolio Management**: Switch between and manage up to 25 distinct client profiles (NGOs, SMEs, Public Bodies) under a single master subscription.
- **White-Label Client Reporting**: Export customized, branded PDF and CSV funding digests with consultancy branding for client distribution.
- **Regional & Municipal Scraper Access**: Coverage of local county and municipal funding sources (e.g., Primăria Cluj-Napoca, Consiliul Județean Cluj, Bihor, etc.).
- **Client Matching Matrix**: Matrix view showing which of the consultancy's 25 clients match a newly published call in real-time.
- **Priority Corrigenda Alerts**: Instant SMS/WhatsApp/Email notifications when guideline changes directly impact a client's active application window.
- **Seats**: Up to 15 agency seats.

---

### Tier 4: Enterprise & Institutional
- **Target Audience**: Large consulting corporations, university research hubs, regional development associations, and hospital networks.
- **Price**: **€799 / month** (or **€7,990 / year**).
- **Target Customer Persona**: Chief Innovation Officers, Directors of European Projects, University Research Deans.

#### Feature Set
- **Unlimited Client & Department Profiles**: Complete organization hierarchy management for large institutions.
- **Custom Scraper Request Pipeline**: Priority development of custom data connectors for niche or specialized national/international funding portals.
- **REST API Access**: Direct API endpoints to ingest cleaned, parsed, and enriched call data into internal ERP, CRM, or custom software systems.
- **Custom AI Model Calibration**: Dedicated tuning of AI match vectors and keyword weighting tailored to institutional grant domains.
- **Dedicated Account Manager & SLA**: 99.9% uptime guarantee, 4-hour support response SLA, quarterly grant intelligence reviews.
- **Seats**: Unlimited seats.

## 5. Architectural Alignment & Data Model Integration

The pricing strategy directly maps to the database schema defined in `supabase/migrations/`:

1. **Multi-Tenancy & Roles**: 
   - Managed via `organizations` and `organization_members` in [0002_organizations.sql](file:///home/workstation/projects/clones/ideas/funding_radar/supabase/migrations/0002_organizations.sql#L22-L41).
   - Agency/Enterprise multi-profile support utilizes single-user linking across multiple org schemas.
2. **Team Triage**: 
   - Managed via `org_call_triage` in [0002_organizations.sql](file:///home/workstation/projects/clones/ideas/funding_radar/supabase/migrations/0002_organizations.sql#L74-L91) supporting statuses: `new`, `relevant`, `applied`, `not_relevant`, `review`.
3. **AI Eligibility Scoring**: 
   - Materialized outputs generated by `match.py` into `org_call_matches` using `profile_hash` invalidation logic.
4. **Idempotent Notifications**: 
   - `org_notifications` in [0002_organizations.sql](file:///home/workstation/projects/clones/ideas/funding_radar/supabase/migrations/0002_organizations.sql#L93-L100) enforces strict non-duplicate notification delivery across all paid tiers.
